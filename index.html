Premium feature
Where the paperwork usually breaks down

Garage Receiving turns the gate into a checkpoint, not a guess.

Every truck and trailer that rolls in is the start of a maintenance record — or the start of a dispute, if nobody wrote it down right. Garage Receiving replaces the gate logbook with a plate-matched, auditable intake that any attendant can run correctly from day one.

No more mystery trailersEvery hitch and unhitch is logged the moment it happens, so you always know what's attached to what — and for how long.
The right vehicle, every timePlate-matched intake stops a truck being logged against the wrong record — no merged histories, no disputed maintenance later.
An audit trail from the first minuteDriver, odometer, timestamp — captured at the gate, not reconstructed from memory during a dispute.
